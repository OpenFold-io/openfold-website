Models & Code

The OpenFold software ecosystem

OpenFold develops state-of-the-art, permissively licensed AI tools for protein and biomolecular structure prediction. The entire training and inference stack, along with training datasets, is released under the Apache 2.0 license, making atomic-accuracy modeling accessible in open source for both research and commercial use.
